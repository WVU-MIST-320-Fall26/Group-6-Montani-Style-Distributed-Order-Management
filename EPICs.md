# Epic Documentation

## Epic 1: DOMS & Legacy Integration (`epic:core-doms`)
**Purpose & Boundary:** High-level backend platform components responsible for centralizing inventory data across all 45 retail stores and e-commerce warehouses. Includes custom API development to connect legacy POS software and the e-commerce engine with real-time sync under 5 seconds.

## Epic 2: BOPIS Engine & Customer Checkout (`epic:bopis`)
**Purpose & Boundary:** Customer-facing e-commerce additions and store pick-up routing logic. Covers real-time store availability widgets during online checkout, automated order routing to local stores, and notifying store associates within 2 minutes of order placement.

## Epic 3: Mobile Store Associate Application (`epic:mobile-app`)
**Purpose & Boundary:** Handheld software deployed across all 45 retail stores. Enables store associates to perform real-time inventory audits, pick and pack BOPIS/ship from store orders directly from shelf stock, and process customer returns on the floor.

## Epic 4: Employee Training (`epic:training`)
**Purpose & Boundary:** Documentation, training modules, and operational rollout protocols designed to onboard store associates within 2 hours of training without disrupting store operating hours.
